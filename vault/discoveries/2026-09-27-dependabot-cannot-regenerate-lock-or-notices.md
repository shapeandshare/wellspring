# Dependabot pip PRs cannot regenerate requirements-lock.txt or third_party_licenses.json

Part of [[wellspring]]. A Dependabot pip bump edits `requirements.txt` only.
The two generated provenance files must still be regenerated by hand, on the
platform they describe.

## What was tested / observed

Read the `lock` and `notices` recipes in the `Makefile`:

- `lock` runs `$(PYTHON) -m pip freeze > requirements-lock.txt.tmp`
- `notices` runs `piplicenses` over the same installed venv

## Finding

Both files are snapshots of **whatever venv is installed**, not a resolution of
`requirements.txt`. So:

- Dependabot has no venv, so its PRs leave both files stale.
- Running `make lock` on a CI runner (Linux, CUDA torch wheel, no `mlx-*`) would
  record a different set than a Track A macOS box. `mlx-vlm` and `mlx-lm` carry
  `sys_platform == "darwin"` markers.

## Relevance

- `.github/dependabot.yml` states the rule in its header comment, and the PR
  template makes it a checklist item.
- Do not add a CI job that auto-commits `make lock` output without first
  deciding which platform the lock describes.
- This is an instance of AGENTS.md §3 (fix at source): edit `requirements.txt`,
  then re-run the generators.
